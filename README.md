## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmBridge is a smart digital agricultural marketplace designed to create a direct connection between farmers and consumers. The platform allows farmers to list their available crops, quantities, expected harvest dates and preferred selling prices. Instead of depending on multiple middlemen, farmers can receive orders directly from households, grocery stores, restaurants and retailers. One special feature of FarmBridge is the Smart Group Order System, which combines orders from nearby buyers so that farmers can supply larger quantities in a single delivery. The platform also provides a price transparency feature that displays the farmer's expected earnings and the buyer's final cost before confirming an order. This solution helps farmers gain better control over their products while allowing consumers to purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices.

## Technical Approach
FarmBridge will be developed as a user-friendly web and mobile-based platform. The frontend can be developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, while Python with Flask or Django can be used for backend development. MySQL or PostgreSQL can store farmer profiles, product details, customer orders, prices and transaction records. The system will include separate dashboards for farmers and buyers, a crop listing module, a smart group ordering module and an order tracking system. A location-based feature can help identify nearby farmers and buyers, while a notification system can inform users about new orders, price changes and delivery updates. The complete process begins when a farmer uploads crop details, followed by buyer selection, group order formation, order confirmation and delivery coordination.

## Feasibility and Viability
FarmBridge is a practical and scalable solution because it uses commonly available technologies and can be implemented without requiring expensive infrastructure. Farmers can access the platform through smartphones, while buyers can place orders using a simple website or mobile application. One possible challenge is that some farmers may have limited digital knowledge or internet access. To overcome this, the platform can provide a simple regional-language interface, easy registration and support through local agricultural groups. Another challenge is transportation and maintaining product freshness. This can be addressed by combining nearby orders, arranging shared delivery schedules and providing advance harvest information. The project can initially be tested with a small group of farmers and local buyers before expanding to larger agricultural markets.

## Impact and Benefits
FarmBridge can create positive economic and social impacts by reducing unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain. Farmers can receive a larger share of the selling price, improve their income opportunities and build direct relationships with buyers. Consumers can access fresh agricultural products at more transparent and reasonable prices. The Smart Group Order System can reduce repeated transportation trips and delivery expenses by combining nearby orders. The platform can also help reduce food wastage by allowing farmers to display upcoming harvests and buyers to place advance orders. In the long term, FarmBridge can support rural development, encourage digital literacy among farmers and create a more transparent and efficient agricultural marketplace.
